Please read the following instructions carefully / कृपया निम्नलिखित निर्देशों को ध्यान से पढ़ें:

  1. Duration & Clock: The examination duration is 90 minutes (1 hour 30 minutes). The countdown timer displayed on the top right shows the time remaining. When the timer reaches zero, the examination will automatically submit.
  2. Question Bank Integrity: All questions and answers strictly originate from official printed answer keys in the provided Practice Sets. Zero questions are hallucinated or generated by AI.
  3. Randomization: Each examination attempt selects 100 verified MCQs via cryptographic Fisher-Yates shuffle. Both question order and option positions are shuffled independently, preserving correct answer mapping.
  4. Question Palette Symbols:
    • Gray: You have not visited the question yet.
    • Red: You have visited the question but have not answered it.
    • Green: You have answered the question.
    • Purple: You have marked the question for review without answering.
    • Purple with Green Dot: The question is answered and marked for review (will be evaluated).
  5. Navigating and Saving:
    • Click "Save & Next" to save your answer and navigate to the next question.
    • Click "Mark for Review & Next" to save your current response (if selected) and flag it for review.
    • Click "Clear Response" to deselect the currently chosen radio option.
    • Click any question number in the Question Palette to jump directly to that question.
  6. Browser Refresh: Refreshing the browser will not lose your progress or reshuffle questions. The active attempt resumes from localStorage automatically.
